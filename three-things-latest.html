Binance Halts Withdrawals After a Security Incident

Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals after what it called a security incident. It put the total impact at about £4.7bn ($6.3bn) and said about £2.3bn ($3bn) had been frozen on-chain. It will cover losses from SAFU, its emergency fund for users, and from company funds. Tether froze more than 1.567bn USDT and Circle froze 823m USDC.

Why this matters

Stablecoin issuers can freeze tokens within hours of an incident. Any bank or payment firm that holds stablecoins, or settles in them, carries that freeze power inside its own balances.

2

Kalshi's Valuation Nearly Doubles in Five Months

Kalshi, the American prediction market, raised £751m ($1bn) at a £30.0bn ($40bn) valuation, nearly double its price five months ago. It claims more than three quarters of prediction-market volume and plans single-stock perpetual futures. Coinbase says its prediction markets now earn more than £150m ($200m) a year.

Why this matters

A prediction market planning single-stock futures competes for the same retail trader as brokers and investment platforms. Their trading revenue now has a rival priced on event contracts.

3

ICE Brings Crypto Perpetual Futures Onshore

Intercontinental Exchange, owner of the New York Stock Exchange, agreed to bring Trade[XYZ]'s perpetual futures, built on the Hyperliquid exchange, onshore in a regulated American market, and is taking a stake. Perpetual futures are contracts with no expiry date, popular on crypto venues. The plan needs regulatory approval.

Why this matters

Perpetual futures would trade in a regulated American market run by the owner of the NYSE. Futures brokers and clearing banks gain a product to offer clients, with the margin and clearing exposure that comes with it.
